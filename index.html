Quick answers

Synapse in simple facts.

Synapse runs online internship and career programs for Indian college students who want practical projects, mentorship, certificates, and placement preparation.

What is Synapse?

Synapse is a career acceleration platform that helps college students learn job-focused skills through structured courses, internship projects, and certificate support.

Which courses are available?

The platform lists AI & ML, Cybersecurity, Aptitude & Placement, and IoT. AI & ML starts from 20 June 2026 with early bird pricing.

Who is it for?

Synapse is designed for Indian college students from first year to final year who want practical learning, portfolio projects, and placement readiness.

What do students get?

Students get course modules, mentor guidance, project outcomes, resume support, mock interview preparation, and a verifiable internship completion certificate.
