Quick answers

Synapse in simple facts.

Synapse runs online internship and career programs for Indian college students who want practical projects, mentorship, certificates, and placement preparation.

What is Synapse?

Synapse is a career acceleration platform that helps college students learn job-focused skills through structured courses, internship projects, and certificate support.

Which courses are available?

The platform lists AI & ML, Cybersecurity, Aptitude & Placement, and IoT. AI & ML starts from 20 June 2026 with early bird pricing.

Who is it for?

Synapse is designed for Indian college students from first year to final year who want practical learning, portfolio projects, and placement readiness.

What do students get?

Students get course modules, mentor guidance, project outcomes, resume support, mock interview preparation, and a verifiable internship completion certificate.

FAQ

Common questions answered.

What is the fee for the internship?
The AI & ML internship is ₹999 (early bird, 80% off from ₹4,999). Other tracks will be priced similarly when they launch.
Is the certificate valid for college?
Yes. You get a verifiable internship completion certificate with a unique ID, project title, and verification link that colleges and recruiters can check.
Do I need prior coding experience?
No. The AI & ML track starts from Python basics. We take you from zero to building ML projects in 45 days.
What is the schedule? Is it live or recorded?
It's a mix of recorded modules + live mentor sessions. You can learn at your own pace with weekly live doubt-clearing and project reviews.
Is there a refund policy?
If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days, you can request a full refund — no questions asked.
How do I get placement support?
You get resume templates, LinkedIn optimization, mock interviews, and project presentation coaching as part of the program.
